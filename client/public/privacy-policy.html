Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 28, 2025

Introduction

Teenz Bible ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

We collect the following information:

  • Account Information: When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture.
  • Usage Data: We track your reading progress, XP points, gems earned, and achievements within the app.
  • Device Information: We may collect basic device information for app optimization.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the app's features (reading progress, leaderboards, achievements)
  • To sync your data across devices
  • To display your profile on leaderboards
  • To improve the app experience

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Firebase services provided by Google. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties. Your leaderboard data (nickname, avatar, XP) may be visible to other users of the app within your class group.

Children's Privacy

Our app is designed for teenagers. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent. If you are a parent and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

Data Deletion

You can delete all your data at any time through the app's Settings page ("Reset All Progress"). You may also contact us to request complete data deletion.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Firebase (Google) - Authentication, database, and hosting
  • Google Cloud Text-to-Speech - For audio Bible reading
  • Google Gemini AI - For Bible AI chat feature

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
