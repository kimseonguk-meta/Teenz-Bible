Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 12, 2025

1. Introduction

Teenz Bible ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your information when you use our web application (the "App"). Our App is designed for teenagers aged 10–18 and is intended to make Bible reading engaging through gamification.

By using the App, you (and your parent or guardian, if you are under 16) agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information

  • Google Sign-In: When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from your Google account.
  • Apple Sign-In: When you sign in with Apple, we receive your name and email address (which may be a private relay address if you choose to hide your email).
  • Anonymous Authentication: If you use the App without signing in, Firebase assigns an anonymous user ID. Your progress is stored under this ID and can be linked to a full account later.

2.2 Profile Information

  • Display name (nickname) you choose
  • Profile photo (uploaded by you or from your sign-in provider)
  • Class/group affiliation

2.3 Usage & Game Data

  • Bible reading progress (books, chapters completed)
  • XP (experience points) and level
  • Gems earned and spent
  • Quiz scores and completion history
  • Daily check-in streaks
  • Achievements and badges
  • Store purchases (pets, themes) using in-app gems
  • Leaderboard rankings

2.4 AI Chat Data

  • When you use the Bible AI chat feature, your questions are sent to Google Gemini AI for processing.
  • Chat history is stored locally on your device and in your Firebase user record to provide conversation continuity.
  • We do not use your chat data to train AI models.

2.5 Feedback Data

  • If you submit feedback through the App, we collect your feedback text, category, and user ID to improve the App.

2.6 Device & Technical Data

  • Browser type and version
  • Device type (mobile, tablet, desktop)
  • Operating system
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token for push notifications (only if you grant permission)

2.7 Local Storage

  • We use browser localStorage to store your preferences (theme selection, language toggle, audio settings) locally on your device. This data is not transmitted to our servers.

4. Data Visible to Other Users

The following information may be visible to other users within your group/crew:

  • Your nickname and profile photo
  • Your XP, level, and leaderboard rank
  • Your reading streak
  • Your achievements

Your email address, quiz answers, AI chat history, and feedback are never visible to other users.

5. Data Storage & Security

  • Your data is stored securely using Firebase Realtime Database and Firebase Authentication, provided by Google Cloud Platform.
  • All data is encrypted in transit (TLS/SSL) and at rest.
  • Access to the database is restricted by security rules that ensure users can only read/write their own data.
  • Profile photos are stored in Firebase Storage with access controls.

6. Data Retention

  • Active accounts: Data is retained as long as your account is active.
  • Inactive accounts: If your account is inactive for more than 24 months, we may delete your data after sending a notification to your registered email.
  • Deleted accounts: When you delete your data, it is permanently removed from our systems within 30 days.
  • Anonymous accounts: Anonymous user data that has not been linked to a full account may be deleted after 12 months of inactivity.

8. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Age Considerations)

Our App is designed for teenagers aged 10–18. We take the following measures:

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent.
  • If a user indicates they are under 13, we require a parent or guardian's email address for consent verification before allowing account creation.
  • Parents or guardians may review, request deletion of, or refuse further collection of their child's information by contacting us.
  • We do not serve targeted advertising to any users.
  • We do not require more information than is necessary to participate in the App's features.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 has provided personal information without your consent, please contact us immediately at the email below.

9. Your Rights

You (or your parent/guardian if you are under 16) have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Update or correct your personal information through the App's profile settings or by contacting us.
  • Deletion: Delete all your data through the App's Settings page ("Reset All Progress") or request complete deletion by contacting us.
  • Portability: Request your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Withdrawal of Consent: Withdraw consent for push notifications through your device settings at any time.
  • Objection: Object to any processing of your data by contacting us.

We will respond to all rights requests within 30 days.

10. Push Notifications

  • Push notifications are optional. You will be asked for permission before we send any notifications.
  • If you grant permission, we store a Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token to deliver notifications (daily reading reminders, achievement alerts, group updates).
  • You can revoke notification permission at any time through your browser or device settings.
  • We do not send marketing or advertising notifications.

11. Location Data

The Bible Map feature displays historical biblical locations on a map. This feature does not collect, access, or store your physical location. All map data is pre-defined historical coordinates and does not require location permissions.

12. Cookies & Local Storage

  • We use browser localStorage to store your app preferences (selected theme, language, audio settings). This data remains on your device only.
  • Firebase Authentication uses cookies/tokens to maintain your login session.
  • We do not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics cookies.

13. Third-Party Services

Our App integrates with the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase (Google) - Authentication, database, hosting, cloud messaging
  • Google Cloud Text-to-Speech - Audio Bible reading
  • Google Gemini AI - Bible AI chat feature

14. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed and stored on servers located outside your country of residence (primarily in the United States and Singapore via Google Cloud). By using the App, you consent to this transfer. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place through our service providers' data protection agreements.

15. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make significant changes, we will:

  • Update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page
  • Notify users through an in-app notification
  • For material changes affecting children's data, we will seek renewed parental consent where required
