Rules to Close the Design Gap

Gold is reward, not wallpaper.

Use gold for CTA, rewards, active tabs, and level moments only.

Every icon must feel branded.

Use thick dark outline, warm gold highlight, sticker-like shape, subtle glow.

Reader stays calm.

Keep the Reader premium and legible. Energy effects happen only on completion.

Luna guides the journey.

Pet is not decoration; it delivers encouragement, reaction, and habit cues.

Motion is part of the brand.

Snappy press depth, XP count-up, reward burst, pet idle, nav bounce.

Faith Quest is the loop.

Read -> XP -> quiz -> gems -> pet reaction -> ranking -> return tomorrow.
