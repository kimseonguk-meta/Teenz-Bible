App Icon DNA

Leather Bible base

Cards and Bible-related UI should borrow dark brown leather, not generic black panels.

Cross-lightning energy

XP and reward moments use the lightning language. Do not use it as the main logo.

Gold corner craft

Use gold bevels for important moments: CTA, level, reward, active state.

Thick dark outline

Icons need sticker-like weight and a dark rim to match the app icon.

Warm yellow glow

Glow is reserved for charged/active/reward states, not every surface.

TB is sacred

Main logo remains the actual app icon. Submarks must never compete with it.

Rules to Close the Design Gap

Main logo is fixed.

The app icon is the brand anchor. No replacement logo.

Icons must inherit app-icon DNA.

Leather, gold bevel, thick outline, glow, sticker depth.

Gold is reward, not wallpaper.

Use for CTA, rewards, active tabs, level moments.

Reader stays calm.

Energy effects happen on completion, not while reading.
