Home hierarchy

  • Start with brand/app status, not decorative chrome.
  • Bible Meme brings fun back into the Home feed.
  • Today’s Faith Quest is the main action card and is driven by last-read state.
  • Quick actions stay secondary and compact.

Implementation mapping

  • HomeStats: flame, XP, gem brand icons.
  • MemeCard: existing Bible Meme of the Day feature.
  • FaithQuestCard: last-read/daily quest logic + reward CTA.
  • QuickActionGrid: Bible AI, Bible Map, Quiz Stats.
  • BottomNav: brand-icon tabs with raised active state.

Quality rules

  • No emoji in Tier 1/2 UI.
  • Gold appears on CTA, reward, active nav only.
  • Reader entry remains one tap away.
  • Hero copy changes by time, last-read state, and quest status.
