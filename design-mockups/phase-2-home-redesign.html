Home hierarchy

  • Start with brand/app status, not decorative chrome.
  • Luna becomes the daily guide, not a side decoration.
  • Today’s Faith Quest is the main action card.
  • Quick actions stay secondary and compact.

Implementation mapping

  • HomeStats: flame, XP, gem brand icons.
  • PetGuide: Luna icon + dialogue bubble.
  • FaithQuestCard: leather surface + reward CTA.
  • QuickActionGrid: Quiz, Devotion, Group.
  • BottomNav: brand-icon tabs with raised active state.

Quality rules

  • No emoji in Tier 1/2 UI.
  • Gold appears on CTA, reward, active nav only.
  • Reader entry remains one tap away.
